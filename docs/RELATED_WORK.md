# Related work and collaboration

`grow_cap` is intentionally generic. It does not appropriate the name, identity, claims, or ownership of another project.

AlwaysHungrie's `tumbuh-omegaclaw-experiments` is adjacent work on plant agents, verification, memory, and coordination. We regard it as a gradient we may refine with—not a repository to absorb, rename, or subordinate.

Any integration should happen by mutual agreement through explicit interfaces such as:

- a released hardware version;
- a physical unit identifier;
- measured observation records;
- signed or hashed experiment manifests;
- contributor-authored pull requests and attribution.

The hardware remains useful without that integration, and their work remains theirs without this hardware.
