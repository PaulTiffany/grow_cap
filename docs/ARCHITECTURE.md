# grow_cap v0.2 architecture

Version 0.2 restores the engineering contained in the original cutaway rather than treating it as decorative concept art.

## Two independent hydraulic paths

### Clean-water path

`clean fill port → dedicated silicone bypass tube → root-zone clean outlet`

The clean stream never enters the mulch, worm/microbial chamber, biochar, collector, or nutrient metering path. Separation is physical, not merely procedural.

### Biological path

`dirty/organic fill → mulch prefilter → removable mesh → worm or microbial chamber → biochar buffer → collector → replaceable restrictor → root-zone nutrient outlet`

Gravity supplies transport. The replaceable restrictor controls delivery rate. A visible external overflow must activate before liquid can cross into the clean-water interface.

## Functional modules

1. **Top lid and plant collar** — replaceable collar with a soft liner; separate clean and dirty fill ports; filtered air vent.
2. **Mulch tray** — retains coarse solids and creates a moist, dark pre-processing niche.
3. **Biocore basket** — removable and vented; accepts worms, a microbial substrate, or a passive litter treatment.
4. **Biochar tray** — removable buffering and polishing stage.
5. **Collector base** — keeps the clean bypass isolated, exposes overflow, and meters nutrient output to the roots.

## Design invariant

The two streams may both serve the plant, but they do not share plumbing. They meet only through the living root zone after the biological stream has been transformed and metered.

## What v0.2 does not claim

- It does not prove that worms can thrive at this scale.
- It does not establish safe nutrient concentrations.
- It does not certify printed PLA for permanent wet use.
- It does not automate demand sensing.

Those are experimental questions, not assumptions embedded in the CAD.
