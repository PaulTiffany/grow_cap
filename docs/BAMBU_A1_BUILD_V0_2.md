# IdeaSpace Bambu Lab A1 build — grow_cap v0.2

## Print order

Do not print the entire stack in one visit before checking fit.

### Plate 1 — interface proof

Export and slice:

- `restrictor` with 2.0 mm orifice
- `restrictor` with 3.0 mm orifice
- a 20 mm-high test section of `biocore` if staff can help crop it in Bambu Studio

Measure the printed holes and wall thickness with calipers.

### Plate 2 — dry stack

- mulch tray
- biocore basket
- biochar tray

Dry-stack these modules and confirm that they separate by hand without wobbling excessively.

### Plate 3 — hydraulic shell

- collector base
- top lid

Only print these after measuring the actual tubing and grommets.

## Bambu Studio baseline

- Printer: Bambu Lab A1, 0.4 mm nozzle
- Process: 0.20 mm Standard
- Scale: 100%
- Walls: 4
- Top/bottom shells: 5
- Infill: 25% gyroid or grid
- Supports: off for all supplied orientations
- Brim: 5 mm for the tall biocore basket if staff recommends it
- Timelapse: off
- Flow calibration: on
- Auto bed leveling: on

Use the filament permitted and supplied by IdeaSpace. PLA is acceptable for dimensional and short-duration dyed-water trials. Do not interpret a successful PLA test as approval for indefinite biological use.

## Orientation

Every module prints upright with its broadest flat face on the bed:

- lid: top face upward
- mulch tray: perforated floor on the bed
- biocore: perforated floor on the bed
- biochar tray: perforated floor on the bed
- base: bottom face on the bed
- restrictor: circular face on the bed

## Required non-printed hardware

Bring or acquire before the hydraulic test:

- two visibly different lengths of flexible silicone tube;
- matching soft grommets for the clean port and nutrient outlet;
- removable coarse mesh;
- clear cups for separate effluent capture;
- blue and amber food coloring;
- calipers;
- a pinch clamp or small inline valve as a backup to printed restrictors.

## First hydraulic test

1. Assemble the stack empty—no worms, compost, fertilizer, or plant.
2. Route the clean bypass tube into a clear cup.
3. Route the biological collector outlet into another clear cup.
4. Add 50 mL blue water to the clean port.
5. Add 50 mL amber water to the dirty port.
6. Record first-drip time and output at 5, 15, and 30 minutes.
7. Fail the test upon any cross-color, hidden pooling, structural leakage, or overflow entering the clean interface.

## Second hydraulic test

Line the biocore with mesh and add washed inert media only. Repeat the test while checking clog visibility and serviceability.

Biology begins only after both hydraulic tests pass.
