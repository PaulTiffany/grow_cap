# Assembly and first validation

## Prototype materials

- two separate lengths of silicone tubing;
- removable seal or grommet for the sump outlet;
- coarse nylon mesh for the basket;
- soft silicone or foam for the plant collar;
- two transparent collection cups;
- blue and amber food coloring;
- calipers and a graduated measuring vessel.

## Dry assembly

1. Seat the clean funnel in the small cap socket and attach its dedicated tube below.
2. Seat the dirty sump in the large cap socket and attach a second dedicated tube.
3. Line the biological basket with coarse mesh and place it inside the sump.
4. Fit the split collar with a soft liner; printed plastic should not abrade a living stem.
5. Route the two tubes to separately labeled transparent cups.

## Gate 0 — fit

Record all actual interface dimensions. Modules must insert and remove by hand without cracking, binding, or uncontrolled free fall.

## Gate 1 — isolation

1. Add 50 mL blue water to the clean funnel.
2. Add 50 mL amber water through the empty mesh-lined basket.
3. Observe both cups and every exposed surface.

**Pass:** each cup receives only its assigned color and every leak is visible and serviceable.

## Gate 2 — gravity

For each path record input volume, time to first drop, output at 5/15/30 minutes, retained volume, and leak location.

## Gate 3 — clog challenge

Test one medium at a time: washed gravel, coarse biochar, dry leaf fragments, then a small amount of finished compost. Repeat flow measurements after each addition.

Do not introduce worms, insects, a valuable plant, sensors, or autonomous control before the passive plumbing passes these gates.
